The control matrix, built from the ground up

There was no program when I arrived — I built it. I designed and implemented the full control matrix across four platforms — AWS, MongoDB Atlas, GitHub, and Azure/Entra — translating SOC 2 and HIPAA requirements into concrete, enforced technical controls on each, then the operational plans around them: business continuity & disaster recovery (BCDR) with RTO/RPO targets, source-code escrow, incident response, data retention, vendor management, and PHI de-identification. The outcome that matters: the platform is independently HIPAA-attested by a third-party AICPA-affiliated firm, and the same control set underpins the SOC 2 Type II observation now in progress (attestation expected within months) — with automated evidence cutting audit-prep time ~70%.

NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment

I authored a formal risk assessment covering 16 assets, 6 threat sources, and 18 vulnerabilities, scored to 2 Critical / 9 High / 8 Medium / 2 Low and mapped to HIPAA safeguards with a prioritized remediation plan and named owners. It turns "we found some issues" into a defensible, board-readable picture of risk — and it set the order in which the application-security and cloud hardening work actually happened. The assessment re-runs annually per HIPAA §164.308(a)(1).

Control mappings

  • HIPAA Administrative (§164.308): security management process, designated security officer, workforce security with same-day offboarding revocation, role-based access, incident response with a severity taxonomy, BCDR with RTO/RPO commitments, and annual evaluation including an external pentest program.
  • HIPAA Technical (§164.312): unique IDs and auto-logoff, AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit, multi-region CloudTrail with log-file validation, application + database + source-control audit trails, integrity controls, and TLS-deny bucket policies for transmission security.
  • SOC 2 Trust Service Criteria (CC-series): CC6 logical access (MFA, RBAC, org scoping), CC7.2 monitoring (CloudWatch, GuardDuty, WAF), CC8.1 change management (PR review, secret scanning on every push).

Evidence automation & policy

I produced 30 audit-ready evidence artifacts in an evidence/ tree (MFA enforcement, branch segregation, PR-review enforcement, user lifecycle, workload identity, WAF rules, API-Gateway schema validation, CloudWatch alarms, and more), each mapped to its SOC 2 CC-series and HIPAA control — reducing manual audit-prep time by ~70%. I also authored a 20+ document policy set (Information Security, Incident Response, Risk Assessment & Treatment, Data Classification, PHI De-identification, BCDR, source-code escrow, Change Management, Vendor Management, SDLC, Access Control & Termination, and the supporting governance charters).

The three tamper-proof audit-log pipelines (GitHub / Entra / Atlas → S3 Object Lock WORM) are the evidence engine behind these controls — immutable proof that the controls operate, not just exist. A compensating-controls package was accepted by the independent third-party assessor.
